# Entry 1: Deciding on which topic to choose
##### 10/19/26

Choosing the topics (photography + videography)

I chose this topic because I've been focused on photography for a long time. Ever since I was little, I've seen a lot of people with their cameras and I've also seen how dedicated photographers are whilst taking their clients photos. The quality and the angles these people would choose always interested me and I've become more focused on their work. Videography is also one of my biggest interests since it captures the live reaction of people at the right time and it creates a huge mood of joy and warmth how one can rewatch the amount of emotion there was in just a video. I had a bunch of fun doing my research on photography. One of these links ["Technology in cameras"](https://rickmcevoyphotography.com/blog/how-technology-in-cameras-helps-us-to-take-photos/) by Rick Mcevoy, showed which cameras are good for photos and how technology works in every single camera one occupies. After reading this link, it made me more passionate about photos and how professional one has to be in order to perfect the photos they really want. I then researched on videography and the history of it. This link ["History of videography"](https://wavesmedia.com/the-evolution-of-video-production-technology/) by Waves media, also drew my attention to this topic, and is the main reason on why I chose this interesting session!
